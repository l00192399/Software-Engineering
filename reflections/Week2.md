## Task 1 - Model Selection Worksheey

1. Aegis Flight-Control Software
Process model: Waterfall
Requirements are stable and clearly understood.
The software has a very high safety risk because defects could endanger lives.
There is a long 12–18 month certification timeline.
Extensive documentation and traceability are required.
Risk: Changes discovered later could be difficult and expensive to make. 

2. Riverside Bakery Marketing Website
Process model: RAD
Requirements are not fully clear at the start.
The owner wants to see working versions and give frequent feedback.
There is a short three-week deadline.
The project has low technical complexity and no safety risks.
Risk: The short deadline and changing requirements could result in a rushed website. 

3. CampusCircle Community App
Process model: Incremental
The founders expect the requirements to change.
They want a usable version within one month.
They want to use feedback from real students to improve the app.
Features can be developed and released in smaller increments.
Risk: Requirements could keep changing and cause the project scope to expand.
